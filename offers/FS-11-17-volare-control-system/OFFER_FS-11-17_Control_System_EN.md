## Subject: Quotation for the supply of a new Control System for Flying Swinger "Volaré" (Job FS-11-17)

Dear Sirs,

Further to your enquiry, we are pleased to submit our best offer for the design, manufacture, supply and commissioning of a **new complete control system** for the Flying Swinger ride **"Volaré"** (our job FS-11-17), installed at Luna Park Sydney, Milsons Point NSW, Australia.

The new control system is designed as a direct replacement of the existing installation and complies with the latest editions of the applicable standards for amusement rides, namely **EN 13814**, **EN 60204-1**, **EN ISO 13849-1** and, as applicable to the installation site, **AS 3533.1 / AS 3533.2** (Australia).

---

## 1. Scope of Supply

### 1.1 Main Control Cabinet

- New main electrical cabinet, painted steel construction, protection degree IP54, complete with anti-condensation heating and thermostatically controlled ventilation / air-conditioning unit suitable for outdoor marine environment (Sydney Harbour location).
- Safety PLC **Siemens SIMATIC S7-1500F** series with fail-safe I/O modules, performing all ride control and safety functions (Performance Level "d", Cat. 3 acc. to EN ISO 13849-1).
- Variable frequency drives **Siemens SINAMICS** series for the main rotation drive motors, complete with line reactors and EMC filters.
- Motor protection, switchgear and control gear (Siemens / Schneider Electric).
- UPS unit for control circuits and safe ride stop in case of mains failure.
- Industrial VPN router for remote diagnostics and tele-assistance from our factory.

### 1.2 Operator Console

- New operator control desk with:
  - colour touch-screen HMI panel **Siemens SIMATIC Comfort** series [12"];
  - hardwired pushbuttons for start / stop / reset, key-operated selector switches (manual / automatic / maintenance mode);
  - emergency stop devices acc. to EN 13850;
  - status and alarm indication lamps.
- HMI application with ride status display, cycle selection, alarm list and log, maintenance counters, multi-language (English / Italian).

### 1.3 Field Equipment

- Set of new position and speed detection devices (absolute encoder on main rotation, inductive proximity switches, zero-position reference).
- Speed and over-speed monitoring implemented in the safety PLC.
- New junction boxes and terminal boxes, IP65, suitable for outdoor marine environment.
- [Wind speed sensor (anemometer) with automatic operating limits – see Options]

### 1.4 Software and Engineering

- Complete new PLC and HMI application software, developed and tested by Preston & Barbieri; ride cycles and operating parameters as per original ride design (Job FS-11-17).
- Safety-related software functions validated acc. to EN ISO 13849-1 / -2.
- Electrical design and schematics produced in EPLAN format.

### 1.5 Documentation (in English)

- Electrical wiring diagrams (as-built);
- Control system operation and maintenance manual;
- Declaration of conformity of the supplied equipment;
- FAT (Factory Acceptance Test) report;
- Commissioning and test report (SAT).
