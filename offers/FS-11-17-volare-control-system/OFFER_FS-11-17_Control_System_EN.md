## Subject: Quotation for the supply of a new Control System for Flying Swinger "Volaré" (Job FS-11-17)

Dear Sirs,

Further to your enquiry, we are pleased to submit our best offer for the design, manufacture, supply and commissioning of a **new complete control system** for the Flying Swinger ride **"Volaré"** (our job FS-11-17, 64 seats), installed at Luna Park Sydney, Milsons Point NSW, Australia.

The new control system is designed as a direct replacement of the existing installation (electrical project FS11_17_AUS, year 2018) and complies with the latest editions of the applicable standards for amusement rides, namely **EN 13814**, **EN 60204-1**, **EN ISO 13849-1** and, as applicable to the installation site, **AS 3533.1 / AS 3533.2** (Australia).

## 1. Reference data of the existing installation

- Supply: 400 V + N, 50 Hz – required power 90 kW – main switch 160 A, Icc 25 kA – control voltage 24 V dc.
- Drives: pole rotation motor 15 kW (inverter duty, with braking resistor), roof rotation motor 30 kW (inverter duty, with braking resistor, supplied through slip-ring collector), hydraulic pump motor 37 kW (soft starter), heat-exchanger fan 0.5 kW, oil heater 0.6 kW.
- Hydraulic power unit (lifting/tilting): proportional directional valve (±10 V reference), No. 7 on/off solenoid valves 24 V dc, No. 3 pressure switches, No. 2 pressure transducers 4–20 mA, oil temperature and level monitoring, emergency descent circuit.
- Safety functions: emergency stop, dual-channel cycle start, motor brakes control, restraint (seat belts / lap-bars) monitoring with belt-opening interlock, over-speed and minimum-speed monitoring, Safe Torque Off on both rotation drives, emergency descent by gravity.
- Panels: main cabinet (1600×2100×600, IP56 outdoor), operator control console, hydraulic unit distribution panel, base and column junction boxes, roof distribution panel with light transformers.

The new control system will reproduce all existing ride functions and operating cycles, so that no re-training of operators beyond the commissioning training is required.

## 2. Scope of Supply

### 2.1 Main Control Cabinet (replacing existing QG)

- New main electrical cabinet, painted steel construction, protection degree IP56, complete with anti-condensation heating and thermostatically controlled air-conditioning unit suitable for outdoor marine environment (Sydney Harbour location).
- Safety PLC **Siemens SIMATIC S7-1500F** series with fail-safe I/O, performing all ride control and safety functions (Performance Level "d", Cat. 3 acc. to EN ISO 13849-1). On request, a like-for-like architecture based on the original platform (Pilz PSS / Schneider) can be quoted as an alternative.
- No. 2 variable frequency drives with Safe Torque Off and braking resistors:
  - pole rotation: 15 kW class, braking resistor ≥5 kW;
  - roof rotation: 30 kW class, braking resistor ≥10 kW.
- Soft starter for the 37 kW hydraulic pump motor; motor starters for heat-exchanger fan and oil heater.
- Main switch 160 A with undervoltage release, earth-leakage protection by external toroid and residual-current relay (0.3 A, <40 ms), dedicated RCDs for light lines and service circuits, as per existing protection scheme.
- Light-lines distribution section (roof, pole, fixed well, lap-bars lighting) with contactors and protections; interface to existing roof light transformers.
- UPS for control circuits and safe ride stop / restraint release in case of mains failure; new 24 V dc regulated power supply.
- Industrial VPN router for remote diagnostics and tele-assistance from our factory (replacing the existing teleservice unit), plus managed Ethernet switch for drives, HMI and PLC network.

### 2.2 Operator Console (replacing existing console)

- New operator control desk, IP66, outdoor rated, with:
  - colour touch-screen HMI panel [12"] with ride status display, cycle selection, alarm list and event log, hour and cycle counters with scheduled-maintenance reminders (as per O&M manual intervals) and exportable event history in support of the log book required by AS 3533.2, multi-language (English / Italian);
  - hardwired devices reproducing the existing operating functions: emergency stop, dual-channel cycle start pushbuttons, key-operated emergency reset, key-operated emergency descent, restraint (safety belts) opening pushbutton, cycle stop pushbutton, horn, restraint/lap-bars status lamp;
  - emergency stop devices acc. to EN ISO 13850.

### 2.3 Hydraulic Power Unit Interface and Field Equipment

- Complete control of the existing Power Hydraulik power unit (Phs3297): proportional valve reference (±10 V), No. 7 solenoid valve outputs, acquisition of No. 3 pressure switches, No. 2 pressure transducers (4–20 mA), oil temperature and level, filter clogging indicators; management of oil heater, cooling fan and emergency descent sequence.
- New hydraulic unit distribution panel (replacing existing UB101).
- Set of new position and speed detection devices on the rotation axes (encoder / proximity switches) as required by the safety functions; speed and over-speed monitoring implemented in the safety PLC.
- Motor brakes control (base, pole, roof) and restraint belt-opening actuators control, maintaining the existing interlocking logic through the slip-ring collector.
- Existing motors, brakes, hydraulic power unit, field sensors, junction boxes and power/signal cabling will be reused subject to inspection at site; new terminal boxes or cables, where found necessary, will be quoted separately.

### 2.4 Software and Engineering

- Complete new PLC and HMI application software, developed and tested by Preston & Barbieri; ride cycles and operating parameters as per original ride design (Job FS-11-17).
- Safety-related software functions validated acc. to EN ISO 13849-1 / -2.
- Electrical design and schematics produced in EPLAN format.
